PORT 07 Questions

Before you order

Do you give a GST invoice?

Yes. Every order comes with a GST tax invoice showing HSN/SAC codes, so you can claim input tax credit.

Can you supply against GeM orders and government tenders?

Yes. We supply government buyers through GeM and e-tenders, with Make in India compliant products and OEM authorisation where the bid asks for it.

What is the difference between comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC?

Non-comprehensive covers engineer visits and labour; spare parts are billed separately. Comprehensive also covers most replacement parts, so your yearly cost is fixed.

How fast do you respond to a breakdown?

Our target is a response within 4 working hours in Gurugram and Delhi NCR and next working day for outstation sites. The exact terms are written into your AMC agreement.

Do you offer laptops and desktops on rent?

Yes, for events, training batches and new projects, monthly or for longer terms.
